On an island, 100 people have blue eyes and the rest have brown. Everyone can see everyone else's eye color but not their own. No one may communicate about eye color. If a person discovers they have blue eyes, they must leave the island at midnight.

A visitor announces: "At least one of you has blue eyes."

## The Surprising Answer

All 100 blue-eyed people leave on the 100th night.

## Why?

With $n$ blue-eyed people:
- If $n = 1$: that person sees no other blue eyes, knows they must be blue, leaves night 1
- If $n = 2$: each sees one blue eye. When that person doesn't leave night 1, they deduce they must also have blue eyes. Both leave night 2
- By induction: all $n$ leave on night $n$
