**Problem:** A coin of radius $r$ is dropped randomly onto a grid of parallel lines spaced 1 unit apart (where $r < 1/2$). What is the probability that the coin crosses a line?

## Solution

This is **Buffon's Needle** problem adapted for a coin. The probability is:

$$P = \frac{2r}{1} = 2r$$

For a penny (radius $\approx 0.75$ cm) on a grid with 2.5 cm spacing:

$$P = \frac{2 \times 0.75}{2.5} = 0.6$$
