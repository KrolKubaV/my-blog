**Problem:** Let $n \geq 2$ be an integer. Find the largest integer $k$ (in terms of $n$) such that there exist $n \times n$ matrices $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_k$ with real entries satisfying $A_i A_j = -A_j A_i$ for all $i \neq j$.

## Hint

Consider the trace of both sides of the anticommutation relation.
