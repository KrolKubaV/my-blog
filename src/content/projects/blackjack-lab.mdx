A lab for learning to count cards properly and for doing blackjack research
in the spirit of Edward Thorp: derive things, prove them by computation, and
practise until they're automatic.

It has three parts:

- **Engine.** A small, tested blackjack engine. Rules are modelled precisely
  (number of decks, S17/H17, DAS, doubling limits, splits, resplitting aces,
  surrender, peek/ENHC, payout, penetration), and shoes are seeded so every
  hand can be reproduced.
- **Practice app.** A Qt6 desktop app with a basic strategy drill, graded
  against rule-specific charts. Every hand is saved together with its exact
  rules.
- **Research notebooks.** Jupyter notebooks for the maths. The first project
  is re-deriving basic strategy from scratch (see the
  [blackjack notes](/notes/blackjack/)).
