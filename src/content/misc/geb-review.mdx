GEB is a mind-bending exploration of mathematics, art, music, and the nature of consciousness.

## Key Ideas

Hofstadter weaves together three seemingly disparate threads:

- **Kurt Gödel's** incompleteness theorems — formal systems cannot prove their own consistency
- **M.C. Escher's** visual paradoxes — self-reference in art
- **J.S. Bach's** fugues — recursive structures in music

## The Central Theme

Strange loops and self-reference are the key to understanding consciousness. The "I" emerges from patterns in the brain that refer to themselves.

## Rating

A masterpiece that rewards multiple readings. Not an easy book, but profoundly rewarding.
