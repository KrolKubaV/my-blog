After years of using various document editors, I've settled on LaTeX for anything involving mathematics.

## Why LaTeX?

- **Typographic quality**: Kerning, ligatures, and spacing are all handled professionally
- **Math support**: No other system comes close for typesetting equations
- **Version control**: Plain text files work beautifully with git
- **Reproducibility**: Same source, same output, forever

## A Simple Example

The quadratic formula in LaTeX:

```latex
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
```

Renders beautifully as:

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$
