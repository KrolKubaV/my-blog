Roulette is a simple game with a mathematical edge that favors the house.

<Definition title="American Roulette">
The wheel has 38 pockets: numbers 1-36, 0, and 00. A bet on a single number pays 35:1.
</Definition>

<Theorem title="Expected Value">
The expected value of a \$1 bet on a single number is:

$$E = \frac{1}{38}(35) + \frac{37}{38}(-1) = -\frac{2}{38} \approx -\$0.0526$$
</Theorem>

<Definition title="European Roulette">
The wheel has 37 pockets: numbers 1-36 and 0 (no 00).
</Definition>

<Theorem title="European Expected Value">
$$E = \frac{1}{37}(35) + \frac{36}{37}(-1) = -\frac{1}{37} \approx -\$0.027$$
</Theorem>

<Remark>
The European wheel has roughly half the house edge of American roulette. Always prefer European when available.
</Remark>
